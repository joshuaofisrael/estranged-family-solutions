Privacy

Privacy note

This is a short notice about the website itself. It is not a full privacy policy and it is not a consent form for therapy.

What this site does

Estranged Family Solutions in West Bloomfield, Michigan publishes this site so people can read about the practice and send an inquiry. The pages are static. They do not include advertising or analytics scripts. Fonts are stored with the site, so viewing a page does not send your address to a font service.

The contact form

Do not send sensitive clinical information through the form. It is not a patient portal.

The form is delivered by FormSubmit, a form service, to the practice inbox. FormSubmit receives your name, email, phone if you include one, the path you are exploring, and your message. The practice does not run its own mail server for this page.

The practice does not sell personal information collected through this website.

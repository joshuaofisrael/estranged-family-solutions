How the two paths differ

Estrangement is a break in contact or closeness, usually after a long strain. The work here starts by telling a wish for repair apart from a need for distance.

Repair & reunite

  • You want some form of relationship, and you can look at what would have to change.
  • Sessions look at pace, language, and what a real apology or a real limit would require.
  • Reconnection is considered only when it can be safe. It is not promised.

Safe distance & coping

  • The relationship is too harmful to keep negotiating, or you have already stepped back.
  • Sessions look at boundaries you can keep, grief, guilt, and safety when safety is the issue.
  • Distance is not treated as a failure of love or of effort.

Who comes to this practice

People arrive from both sides of a family break. The first task is to understand the relationship you actually have, not the one a holiday card implies.

Adult children

You may be limiting contact with a parent because closeness has meant criticism, control, chaos, or a role you were too young to carry. You can miss them and still be right to protect yourself.

Parents

You may be facing an adult child’s silence or limits. Therapy can help you understand what repair would require, without treating their boundary as a problem to get around.

Other family

Siblings and other adult relatives caught in a cutoff are welcome. The question is the same: what kind of relationship, if any, can be honest and safe.

A first conversation

You do not need a finished decision, a diagnosis, or a polished explanation of your family.

  1. Read the closer path

    Start with repair or with distance, even if you are unsure. Each page says who the work is for, and when it is the wrong work.

  2. Send a short note

    Say which path you are exploring, or that you have not decided. Add the way you prefer to be reached.

  3. Use the first meeting as a fit check

    Ask how the therapists work. A first conversation is not a promise of reconciliation, and this website is not an emergency service.

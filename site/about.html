The practice

Sherry Wexler and Marcie Israel

Licensed therapists in the state of Michigan. Estranged Family Solutions is their practice in West Bloomfield, focused on family estrangement.

What this practice is

The work is limited on purpose. This is not a general counseling office that lists estrangement among twenty specialties. Adults come here for one of two paths: repair and reunite, when a healthier relationship may be possible, or safe distance and coping, when it is not.

The practice is local to West Bloomfield, Michigan. Ask how sessions are held, and about current availability, when you get in touch. This site does not describe a telehealth program, insurance panel, or fee schedule the practice has not published.

Marcie provides therapy through this practice for adults affected by family estrangement, including parents and adult children. Sessions are a place to sort what reconnection would require, and what to do when it is not appropriate.

About credentials. Both therapists are licensed to practice in Michigan. This website does not list degrees, license numbers, or years in practice. Those details should come from the practice when you ask, not from marketing copy. Please request licensure information when you inquire.

How the work is bounded

  • We do not promise a reunion, and we do not treat distance as a moral failure.
  • We will not help you pressure, monitor, or punish a relative who has asked for space.
  • We will not tell you that staying is always braver, or that leaving is always the cure.
  • Coming to therapy is not the same as bringing your whole family into the room. If more than one person wants to attend, ask. This site does not describe a joint-session or mediation program.

Sessions are for the adult who comes in. Parents are welcome as clients. This practice is not described here as therapy for children.
